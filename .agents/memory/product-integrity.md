---
name: Product integrity
description: Non-negotiable product scope and honesty requirements from the user's Ethio HUDHUD brief.
---

Ethio HUDHUD teaches Amharic only. Arabic is the default interface and teaching
language; do not expand into Oromo or Tigrinya.

**Why:** The user's product brief explicitly limits the language scope.

**How to apply:** Keep curriculum, tutor instructions, marketing and future
extensions within the Amharic-learning product.

Organize the learning programme for six months with beginner, intermediate and
advanced stages, including practical lessons and conversations, next/back
navigation and tracked lesson completion.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this as the organisation of the Learn page.

**How to apply:** Keep future curriculum additions consistent with that study
path rather than turning the catalogue into an unstructured list. Treat the
duration as a suggested study schedule, not a guarantee of fluency.

Include number lessons from 0 to 100 throughout the six course months, showing
the numeric value, Amharic word and Arabic-script pronunciation (for example,
0 · ዜሮ · زيرو).

**Why:** The user explicitly requested number lessons across all course months.

**How to apply:** Keep numerical learning progressive across the six-month path,
with practical contexts and the same quizzes and completion tracking as other lessons.

Pronunciation guides for Amharic words and letters must use Arabic script
throughout the app, not Latin transliteration.

**Why:** The user says the learners are Arabic-language speakers and need to
read how Amharic words and letters are pronounced.

**How to apply:** Apply this to all learning surfaces, alphabet examples,
revision cards, quiz explanations, tutor instructions and admin-authored content.
Keep pronunciation distinct from the Arabic translation and label it approximate.

Do not present unreviewed material as verified, invent tutor listings or audio,
fabricate pronunciation assessment, or claim a paid entitlement from a button.

**Why:** The brief explicitly requires reviewer-approved content, real providers
and verified payment webhooks rather than placeholder production features.

**How to apply:** Preserve internal review status and require genuine linguistic
review and actual provider support before claiming approval or availability.
Do not show the recurring learner-facing “This is pilot material awaiting
qualified review. Lessons may contain mistakes.” notification or its Arabic
equivalent; the user explicitly requested its removal throughout the app.
Keep factual audio limitations and approximate-pronunciation guidance.

The requested billing provider is Polar.sh. Do not silently substitute another
provider or activate Premium without verified payment.

**Why:** The brief names Polar as the subscription provider.

**How to apply:** Keep paid access disabled until the requested integration can
be configured and verified, or the user explicitly approves a different provider.
